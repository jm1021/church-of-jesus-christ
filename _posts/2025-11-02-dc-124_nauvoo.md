## Historical Context

Key Themes:

Enduring persecution with faith.

The Lord’s timing and compassion: “My son, peace be unto thy soul; thine adversity and thine afflictions shall be but a small moment.” (v. 7)

True priesthood power flows from righteousness, not control or compulsion (vv. 34-46).

### Section 121

**Light in the Darkness: Liberty Jail**  [www.churchofjesuschrist.org](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/learn/history/sites/missouri/light-in-the-darkness?lang=eng)
[![Liberty Jail](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/8/87/Liberty_Jail_by_C.C.A._Christensen.png)](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/learn/history/sites/missouri/light-in-the-darkness?lang=eng)

**Backdrop**: The Saints are fleeing Missouri as a result of* the extermination order.

**Setting**: Prophet Joseph Smith and companions imprisoned in Liberty Jail, Missouri (March 1839).

**Content**: Prayer, lament, and revelation on suffering, patience, and priesthood power.

- 7 [My son, peace be unto thy soul;](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/dc-testament/dc/121?lang=eng&id=p7#p7)

- 33 [As well might man stretch forth his puny arm to](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/dc-testament/dc/121?lang=eng&id=p33#p33)

- 34 [Behold, there are many called](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/dc-testament/dc/121?lang=eng&id=p34-p36#p34)

- 35 [Let thy bowels also be full of charity](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/dc-testament/dc/121?lang=eng&id=p45-p46#p45)

**Official Heading** Prayer and prophecies excerpted from two epistles to the Church dictated by Joseph Smith the Prophet while he was a prisoner in the jail at Liberty, Missouri, the first dated March 20, 1839, and the second composed around March 22. The Prophet and several companions had been months in prison. Their petitions and appeals directed to various government officials had failed to bring them relief.

### Section 124

**Old Nauvoo Temple**  [www.churchofjesuschristtemples.org](https://churchofjesuschristtemples.org/nauvoo-temple/)

[![Old Nauvoo Temple]({{site.baseurl}}/images/nauvoo_temple_old.jpg)](https://churchofjesuschristtemples.org/nauvoo-temple/)

**Setting**: Revelation given January 19, 1841 in Nauvoo, Illinois, after the Saints’ relocation.

**Backdrop**: Saints are rebuilding after persecution — establishing a new home and headquarters.

**Content**: Commandment to build:
Clairty of terms.

- The Nauvoo Temple — “a house unto my name”
- The Nauvoo House — a public house for visitors and gathering.

Scriptures.

- 22 [House for boarding](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/dc-testament/dc/124?lang=eng&id=p22-p23#p22)
- 27 [House in my name, for the Most High](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/dc-testament/dc/124?lang=eng&id=p27-p32#p27)
- 37 [Washing, Baptisms for the Dead](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/dc-testament/dc/124?lang=eng&id=p37-p41#p37)
- 58 [Abrahamic promise to Joseph and his seed](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/dc-testament/dc/124?lang=eng&id=p58-p59#p58)
- 91 [Hyrum and the office of Partiarch](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/dc-testament/dc/124?lang=eng&id=p91-p93#p91)
- 124 [Priesthood Offices](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/dc-testament/dc/124?lang=eng&id=p124-p129#p124)

**Official Heading** Revelation given to Joseph Smith the Prophet, at Nauvoo, Illinois, January 19, 1841. Because of increasing persecutions and illegal procedures against them by public officers, the Saints had been compelled to leave Missouri. The exterminating order issued by Lilburn W. Boggs, governor of Missouri, dated October 27, 1838, had left them no alternative. In 1841, when this revelation was given, the city of Nauvoo, occupying the site of the former village of Commerce, Illinois, had been built up by the Saints, and here the headquarters of the Church had been established.

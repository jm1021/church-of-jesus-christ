## **Living Witness**

My topic today is **to be a Living Witness**. In my recent reading and in conversations with Lora, I have begun to see three ideas intertwine:

**Living Witness**,
**Living Water**, and
**the Living Christ**.

My initial sense is that becoming a *Living Witness* is, at its heart, a **process of enlightenment**—a slow dawning of spiritual understanding that changes us over time.

---

## **Artistic Symbols of Enlightenment**

In art, the **halo** or circle of **light**—something external, usually above the head—symbolizes **glory**, **awakening**, **wholeness**, and **divine power**.
It visually testifies of a person’s spiritual state.

Two examples:

1. **Leonardo’s *Virgin of the Rocks***
   In the London version, halos surround Mary, the infant Jesus, and the infant John the Baptist—foreshadowing their divine ministries.

2. **Giotto’s *Last Supper***
   Jesus bears a gold halo, the apostles silver ones—symbolizing the light Christ extends to His disciples.

Sacred narratives mirror this same symbolism of light descending:

1. **The Burning Bush** — divine light preparing Moses for his calling.
2. **The First Vision** — a pillar of light surrounding the Father and the Son as They prepare Joseph Smith for the Restoration.

These moments show a consistent pattern:
**Light descends. Transformation begins.**

---

## **The Science of Enlightenment**

Modern noetic science studies human consciousness by blending objective tools with subjective spiritual experience.
The word *noēsis* means **direct inner knowing**—another form of enlightenment.

This parallels what the Spirit does for us spiritually:
**revelation that enlightens both mind and heart.**

---

## **Moroni 10:4 — The Divine Formula for Enlightenment**

Moroni teaches that if we ask God with a sincere heart, real intent, and faith in Christ, the **Holy Ghost will manifest truth** to us.

This manifestation is the spark—
the **Living Water** beginning to flow.

## **Personal Experiences as a Living Witness**

Below are some of the experiences that formed my understanding of the **Living Christ**, the **Living Water**, and what it means to become a **Living Witness**.

---

### **Early Impressions of Light — Becoming a Member of Record**

After baptism, something awakened in me. I didn’t have language for it then, but it felt like *noēsis*—an inner awareness that the commandments mattered.
Whenever I stepped outside them, I **felt** it.

I also had men who quietly ministered to me for years—Lance Wickman and Roger Hawley. Their lives radiated a steady spiritual light.
Looking back, I recognize that in them I saw what I now call a **living halo**—a reflection of the Living Christ in an ordinary disciple.

During these years, activities with friends, sports, and youth programs kept me just close enough to “dip my toes” into the water.

---

### **College — A Turning of the Soul**

College was full:

* a heavy class load,
* practicing basketball with the college team,
* the long brown hair of the late ’70s.

But despite the excitement, something in my soul felt **aggravated**—like my life was out of spiritual alignment.

At the end of my first year, I turned instinctively to the **Book of Mormon**.
I already knew Moroni’s promise, so with real intent I read for many weeks.

I was seeking the **Living Christ**.
What I received was the **Living Water**.

---

### **The Living Water — Moroni’s Promise Fulfilled**

> *“He will manifest the truth of it unto you by the power of the Holy Ghost.”*
> — Moroni 10:4–5

My witness is this: **that manifestation is the Living Water.**

After I asked with all my heart, the change came quickly.
Not just spiritually—
**physically, emotionally, and socially—something shifted.**

Within days:

* three of my high school friends and I found ourselves united in faith,
* an old girlfriend and I reconnected with new purpose,
* each of us independently chose to serve missions.

The Living Water didn’t just fill me—
it **united** us.

---

## **On My Mission — Watching the Water Flow Into Others**

Missionary work allows you to see the Living Water come alive in others.

In **King’s Lynn** and **Wisbech**, I witnessed Pentecostal-like experiences:

* A dark road, a last house, a simple testimony—and a family visibly changed in one evening.
* A remote farmhouse, *no logical reason to find it or knock*, yet the Spirit led us there.
* A woman who rejected us at the door, then ran after us fifteen minutes later calling us back for her and her children.

We went from zero people to teach to about twenty in a few weeks.
It was a clear witness that the **Living Christ** was actively gathering His children.

---

### **Returning 40 Years Later — A Generation of Living Witnesses**

In 2015, I returned to that area—**forty years later**.

I saw a generation that had grown from those early conversions.
It felt like seeing Pentecost in reverse—observing what the Living Water had done over four decades.

One man we taught—now serving in a bishopric—joined us on the visit to our old ward.
We reunited with a former branch president and saw photographs and memories stretching across time.
So many lives changed from distant spiritual events.

The Spirit confirmed to me again:

**The Living Christ is real.
His Living Water changes lives.
And over time, we become Living Witnesses of His work.**

---

## **Living in the Gospel — And Becoming a Witness**

Not everything in the gospel is understood immediately.
The Living Water flows constantly, but we do not always understand its course.

During my years in LA:

* surfing,
* playing basketball,
* my Interceptor 500,
* early tech career…

…I kept the Living Christ somewhere in sight, but life was busy.

Then one moment on Catalina Island changed everything.
I had risen from spearfishing, looked across a field, and saw a perfect bump–set–spike.
The spike came from **Tamara**.

That moment turned my head—
and began a new story.

---

### **Meeting a Living Witness — Tamara**

We talked, became close, and soon she and her cousin moved into our ward.
We sang in church, shared testimonies, and life felt nearly perfect.

Then came her diagnosis:
**Hodgkin’s Lymphoma.**
She returned to Texas for treatment.

Six months later, we decided to get married.
I stayed with another family while she moved into my place as we prepared for the temple.

But the medical news grew worse.
The doctors said there was **no progress**.

Whole families fasted.
Primary children prayed for her.
At the veil ceremony, Tamara wrote that she felt a sudden **quickening**—something spiritual and real.

After our honeymoon, the doctors told us:

**There was no cancer. Hodgkin’s was gone.**

But the treatment meant she could not have children.

---

### **Miracles of Living Water — Our Family Begins**

We moved to Eugene, Oregon.
A patriarch advised us to *be patient* with the promises in our blessings.

But the Lord didn’t wait long:

* **Trent**
* **Corey**
* **Allaya** (for a year)
* **Claire**

Child after child came into our lives through adoption and the Living Christ’s timing.

Tamara and I shared nearly twenty years together.
She was a **Living Witness** in everything—faith, testimony, joy, service.

---

### **Her Final Witness**

A second cancer came: **Mesothelioma**.
As I blessed her, a memory returned—the missionary experience where we blessed a man who was being called home.

I knew.
That evening, Tamara and I talked.
We hoped and prayed—but the Lord took her quickly.

And I received a sure spiritual witness that she was welcomed in glory—whole, radiant, received by the Christ she had testified of all her life.

She was, to me:

* **a Living Witness of the Living Christ**,
* a vessel of **Living Water**,
* and a reminder that this path is *real*.

Through her, I often saw Him.

---

## **Living Water Continues**

After Tamara passed, I was left raising a 16-year-old, a 15-year-old, and a 7-year-old as a widower.

In grief, the veil is thin.
I had tender mercies—a dream of the Savior, and a moment when Tamara stood at my bedside in unmistakable reality.

But my experiences with the Spirit did not always match my children’s experiences.
Being a **Living Witness** for them was hard.
They had their own questions, their own grief, their own lenses.

And yet, the Lord provided miracles:

* A one-in-a-million au pair from France—a returned missionary, full of light—brought stability into our home.
* After a year, life opened again.
  I found myself dating—sometimes feeling 46, sometimes 25.
  Trent, Corey, and I even “co-dated” through West Coast Swing and Salsa lessons.

The Living Water kept moving forward.

---

### **A New Miracle — Lora**

Then the Living Christ offered another miracle: **Lora**.

In the Celestial Room of the San Diego Temple, something changed.
I saw her differently—truly presented to me as a gift, a new angel in my life.

A later temple visit and a car ride confirmed that the Lord was still working miracles.
That night I literally shouted “hooray” through my sunroof.

Lora and I have now shared 19 years of adventures.
These years brought **Tiernan** to me and **Shay** to us.
In 2027, Shay will graduate—marking the conclusion of **40 years of parenting minors**.

I shifted from corporate tech into full-time teaching.
I’ve now taught seven years—and have been blessed to have Shay as a student for nearly two of them.
I have also been fortunate to see the blessings of education in many of my students as they progress in their lives.

The miracle of finding Lora, forming a new family, and watching my new life unfold has taught me this:

**The Lord has been increasing my capacity to be a Living Witness.**

---

### **Looking Forward**

Life changes directions, but my center remains:

**The Living Christ**
and
**His Living Water**.

Lora and I hope to celebrate our 20th anniversary in Europe.
We hope to serve a mission in 2027.
I hope Shay will serve too.

And I hope all my children—even in their doubts—will choose to become **Living Witnesses** in their own unique way.

Because they already are.
Every one of them.
Their lives, goodness, compassion, and growth testify of God’s hand.
I see it. I feel it.

And Lora…
you are a special part of this story.
A miracle.
A partner.
A witness of Christ’s love in my life.

---

## **My Testimony**

**Life is good with Jesus in the middle.**

Christ shapes us through growth, loss, pruning, and renewal.

Not every garden grows in a straight row.
Even the most fertile, beautiful garden is sometimes pruned—not as punishment, but as preparation.

Seasons of desert.
Seasons of water.
Seasons of bloom.
Seasons of cutting back, so that something new can grow.

Not linear. Not simple. But deeply, divinely shaped.

and become a **Living Witness** of His goodness.

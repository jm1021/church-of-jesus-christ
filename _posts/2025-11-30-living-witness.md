## Living Witness

My topic today is **to be a Living Witness**. In my recent reading and in conversations with Lora, I’ve begun to see three ideas become intertwined—**Living Witness**, **Living Water**, and the **Living Christ**.

My initial sense is that becoming a *Living Witness* is, at its heart, a **process of enlightenment**.

---

### Artistic Symbols of Enlightenment

In art we often see the **halo** or **light**—something external, usually above the head—representing meaningful symbols such as *glory*, *enlightenment*, *the circle*, and *power*. These visual elements point to a person’s elevated or awakened spiritual state.

The halo, that circle of radiant light, is an artistic representation of spiritual character or glory. We see this symbol used across many depictions of enlightened or holy figures throughout history:

1. **[Leonardo’s *Virgin of the Rocks*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Virgin_of_the_Rocks)**.  
   In the two existing versions, the London version includes halos for Mary, the infant John the Baptist, and the infant Jesus—an artistic foreshadowing of divine enlightenment in Christ’s coming ministry.

2. **[Giotto’s *Last Supper*](https://www.wikiart.org/en/giotto/last-supper)**.  
   Here, Jesus is shown with a gold halo, and the apostles with silver halos—a symbolic preview of Christ extending His enlightenment to His disciples.

A similar symbolism of **light descending from above** is found in sacred narratives:

1. **[The Burning Bush](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Burning_bush)**, where the word of the Lord comes to Moses before he is called to lead Israel.
2. **[The First Vision](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/First_Vision)**, where a pillar of light encompasses the Father and the Son as They appear to Joseph Smith before the restoration of the Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints.

These artistic and scriptural moments all reflect the same pattern: **light descends, and transformation follows.**

### Science of Enlightenment

Noetic science explores a similar idea but from a modern, multidisciplinary perspective. It blends objective scientific tools with subjective inner knowing to study consciousness, human experience, and the interconnectedness of reality.  
The term comes from the Greek *noēsis*, meaning *inner wisdom* or *direct knowing*—another form of enlightenment.

---

### Moroni 10:4 — The Promise of Enlightenment

Moroni 10:4 offers a clear method for gaining spiritual understanding: ask God, with a sincere heart, real intent, and faith in Christ. The promise is that the **Holy Ghost will manifest truth**, illuminating the mind and heart. It is, in essence, a divine formula for enlightenment.

### A Living Witness

As a missionary, there were pentecostal experiences, specifically in Kings Lynn and Wisbech.

The last house down and dark road, a response to the door, baring witness of Heavely Father and Jesus Christ.  Seeing others enveloped in the Living Water, an evening transformation so powerful that they start the process of change.

Another day in the country, what seemed like going to a way-out-of the way farmhouse and finding a family and many children who recently relocated to the area.  There was no internet, but we felt led to a powerful gospel family.

Or an evening where a lady rejected us at the door.   But, about 15 minutes later as we were about to do away from tracting in vain, the lady came running down the street and asked us to come back to her and her three children.

It was an amazing and formidable witness for me in the Living Christ. He wanted us to discover and withing a few weeks we went from 0 to about 20.

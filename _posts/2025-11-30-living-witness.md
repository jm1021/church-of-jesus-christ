### Artistict Symbolos of Englightenment

In art we see the Halo or Light, something external, often above the head that represents the meaninful symbols "glory", "enlightenment, "the circle" and "power" all which depict englightenment.

The halo, which is a circle of light, is an artistic representation of a person's spiritual character or glory. We see this symbol in many artistic representations of enlightened historical figures.

1. [Leonardo's Virgin on the Rocks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Virgin_of_the_Rocks).  The two version can be reviewed on Wikipedia, the London version has a Halo for Mary, and for the infant John the Baptist and the infant Jesus.  A preview of the englightment of Christ's ministry.
2. [Giotto's Last Supper](https://www.wikiart.org/en/giotto/last-supper) shows a Gold Halo for Jesus and a Silver Halo for each of the apostles.  A preview of Christ transitioning world enlightment to the aposles.

A pillar of light or transforation are depictied in the First Vision

1. [A Burning Bush](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Burning_bush) proceeds the word of the Lord (Yahweh) prior to directing Moses to lead the children of Israel.
2. [A Pillar of Light](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/First_Vision) encompasing the Father and the Son as the appeared to Joseph Smith prior to leading him to restore the Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints

### Science of Englightenment

Noetic science is a multidisciplinary field that combines objective scientific tools with subjective inner knowing to study consciousness, human experience, and the interconnectedness of reality. The term comes from the Greek word noēsis, meaning "inner wisdom" or "direct knowing".

### Moroni 10:4 Promise of Englightenment

Moroni 10:4 describes a method for receiving a testimony of truth, particularly the Book of Mormon, by asking God in prayer with a sincere heart, real intent, and faith in Christ. The verse promises that the truth of these things will be manifested by the power of the Holy Ghost.
